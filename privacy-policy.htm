Privacy Policy for RekomPro

Last updated: March 2026

Information We Collect

RekomPro connects to Pinterest API to help users manage product recommendations from Shopee. We only access:

  • Pinterest board information (with your explicit permission)
  • Basic Pinterest profile information (username, email)

How We Use Your Information

We use Pinterest API access to:

  • Create and manage boards for product categories
  • Create and schedule pins from Shopee product links
  • Organize pins into board sections

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties. Shopee affiliate links are included in pins as part of the app's functionality.

Data Security

We use OAuth 2.0 authentication and do not store your Pinterest credentials. All API calls are made securely.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: privacy@rekompro.com
